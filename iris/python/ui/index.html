SETTINGS & HELP

Your data, your choice.

This local research demo stores a protected iris record only after you consent.

Local app access

Enter the access key from the PowerShell terminal running the local server. It stays in memory in this tab and is cleared when you close it.

No access key entered.

Privacy

  • Camera images are processed in memory and are not saved by this app.
  • A protected iris record is stored encrypted on the local server after registration.
  • Your account identifier is transformed in this browser before it is sent. This is pseudonymous, not anonymous.
  • This demo has no liveness detection. A photo or replay may fool it; do not use it to protect an account or device.
  • Only register yourself or another person with informed consent. Check local laws and dataset terms before collecting or testing biometric data.

Delete a registration

Enter the same account identifier used to register. This removes its protected record from the local database.

Troubleshooting

Camera preview is blank

Allow camera access in your browser, close other camera apps, or choose an image instead. This demo needs a clear, close eye image.

It says registration already exists

Use Verify identity, or delete the existing registration before registering again.

Verification is unsuccessful

Try a bright, clear eye image without sunglasses. This research demo is not calibrated for real identity decisions.
